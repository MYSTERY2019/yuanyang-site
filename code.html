FateLens

An AI-powered BaZi and Zi Wei Dou Shu charting tool. Apart from the AI reading, everything is computed on the phone. No network access, no birth data uploaded: the whole engine sits on the device.

Four pillars, ten gods and hidden stems on one screen. Tap a pillar for the detail, tap a palace for its stars and transformations. Coordinates for over a thousand cities give true solar time from the birthplace longitude, for accurate charting.

Android / web · fatelens.cc.cd

Yearly luck rolls from the ten-year cycle down to the day and hour, with a one-line reading and seven chapters generated on-device, so you can read your own chart in full. Two-person charts score day stem, day branch, five elements and Zi Wei star interactions; with the AI service, you can ask anything about the reading.

Charting is free, and the AI reading has a free daily allowance. Available as an Android app and on the web.
